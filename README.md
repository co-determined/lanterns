# Lanterns

Tap the lanterns as they rise. Don't let them drift away.

A small warm browser game — one file, no dependencies, works on a phone. Pick a mode: *before bed*, *afternoon slump*, *calming down*, *waiting room*. Each one is tuned for where you are; the before-bed one gets slower and warmer as you play, and the score quietly stops mattering.

Nothing leaves your device. The one-tap "how do you feel?" at the end is stored in your own browser's localStorage and nowhere else.

Play: https://co-determined.github.io/lanterns/
